Ayush YadavSenior Executive · EHS

I believe safety is not just a checklist. It is a way of thinking, communicating and looking after people.

I currently manage EHS for municipal solid waste collection and transportation in Rewa and Satna, working with the Rewa Municipal Corporation at Renvion Solutions.

The waste collected by these operations goes to a Waste-to-Energy plant that supplies about 6 MW of electricity to the grid. My work covers fleet and road safety, driver and helper safety, risk assessment, incident and near-miss investigation, PPE compliance, inspections, toolbox talks, emergency preparedness, contractor safety and environmental compliance.

Earlier, I worked on industrial construction and MEP projects, including a Siemens manufacturing facility and a Microsoft data centre.
